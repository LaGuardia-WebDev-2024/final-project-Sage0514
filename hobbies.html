Singing

I have been singing for about seven or eight years at this point. I started in second grade in a church choir and then proceeded since then. I was homeschooled for most of my life so this gave me a lot fo time to train and develop technique and my own style. I now incorporate this into songs I write now and will hopefully release in the near future.

Piano + Guitar

I've been playing piano for about eight years now. It was my first ever instrument and where I do most of my songwriting. Although, I am beginning to experiment with the guitar and creating songs with that as well.

Writing

I used to despise writing and was actually scared of it. I never thought I could be a good writer until I started getting older and actually diving deep into what I want to express through my writing. Now it is a huge comfort and coping mechanism for me. Whenever I feel anxious or down in the dumps I just start writing my poetry and see what I create purely with my emotions.

Bible Reading

I'm trying to do the challenge of read the bible in a year. So I try to read at least three chapters a day. It has been really interesting because I have learned a lot of context I had no clue was there. I know this is something that benefits my mental health and relationshi0p with God so I am very grateful I started this journey.
